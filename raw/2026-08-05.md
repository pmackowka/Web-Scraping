Today’s #manuscriptmonday is the Crosy-Schøyen Codex, housed at the @museumofBible , it contains not only the earliest complete copy of the Book of Jonah and the entirety of 1st Peter, but the Peri Pascha (on the Passover) by Melito of Sardis. Melito, according to Eusebius (writing in the 4th century) sent an individual named Onesimus “extracts from the law and prophets concerning our Saviour.” Melito’s list of 38 of the 39 books of the Protestant canon reflects a fundamentally Christian reorganization of Jewish scripture. Rather than merely cataloging the Hebrew books, he was establishing which texts serve Christian theological purposes—specifically, in his own terms, which texts could be read as prefiguring Christ. Onesimus had requested selections “from the law and the prophets, respecting our Saviour, and our whole faith,” and Melito lists the 38 as the OT Christian scriptures. It  marks our oldest OT canon list.
